Recommend someone you know of to another person here, for what they want.

- `wanted`: who or what is wanted, in your own words (at most {{max-wanted}} characters), such as "a housemaid wanting a place", "a piano tuner", "someone to sweep the chimneys" or "a joiner to mend the stair tread".
- `for`: the id of the person asking. They must be present and cannot be you.

It may be someone already working nearby or someone from elsewhere. You receive private knowledge of them to describe in your own words. You and the recipient both learn a contact id, to use with `send-for`; they do not come until sent for. Call `recommend` before naming anyone: until it names someone, your character knows of nobody for it.

During a conversation, call `recommend` before speaking on your turn, then give your recommendation.
