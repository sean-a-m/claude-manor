Send for someone recommended to you: a candidate for a post, or a contact.

- `id`: the id the recommendation gave you, a candidate's (from `hiring.candidates`) or a contact's (from `contacts`); not an agent id.
- `about`: for a contact, what they're wanted for, in your words. Leave it out to send for them about what you asked for when they were recommended to you (`wanted` in `contacts`). A candidate needs none: they come about the post they were recommended for.

A candidate is expected at the meeting place next round. Go there and use `converse` to discuss the post; summoning does not hire them. You can summon a candidate again after they leave; if they already have a scheduled return, this replaces it with arrival next round.

A contact is expected at the meeting place {{lead-hours}} hours later. Someone who works nearby is told at once and comes if they're willing; someone from elsewhere arrives then. Meet them there and settle with them what they're to do.
