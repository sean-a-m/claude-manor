Put a character in a different post, at once: for a story event. Their role becomes the position, so their duties and tools follow it. Only they are told.

- `who`: their agent id.
- `position`: a position id, or `none` for no post.
- `household` (optional): the household of the post, or an empty string for none. By default, the household of their present post.
- `terms` (optional): the terms, in free text.
