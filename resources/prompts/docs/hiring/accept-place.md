Accept your current offer and take the post immediately. The person who made the offer must be present with you.

- `conditions` (optional): free-text conditions recorded with your accepted post. This still accepts the offer; it is not a counteroffer awaiting approval.

To negotiate first, discuss the terms before accepting. If the offerer is elsewhere, use `find-person` and `walk` to reach them, or ask them to come.

During a conversation, call `accept-place` before speaking on your turn.
