End the visit of someone sent for who holds no post (an unengaged candidate, or anyone who came when sent for) and send them home, out of the mapped area.

- `who`: the agent id of someone present with you, not their candidate or contact id.

This closes any offer to them. Someone who knows their candidate or contact id through a recommendation can send for them again with `send-for`.

This does not dismiss hired staff. It ends your turn.
