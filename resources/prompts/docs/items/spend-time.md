Keep doing to at most {{advised-doing}} characters. `hours` is half an hour to {{max-work-hours}}, default is half an hour. Optionally name up to {{max-work-items}} things or people with {{on}}: items here or carried, this place's id, stores in scope, or the ids of people here, yourself included. Working on a container includes its contents. `spend-time` only counts the amount of time that was completed without interruption. If you `spend-time` on a person and they `walk` away, or if someone uses `rouse` to stop you before the end of the duration, only the time you worked counts. {{act-on}}You are occupied with the `spend-time` task until the end of the duration and learn the results once you finish.
