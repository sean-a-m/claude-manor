Check whether this store can sell requested portable goods and quote firm prices to a customer here. Use for (the customer's id) and goods: a list of up to {{max-lines}} lines, each one kind of goods with item and amount, how much of it, as the customer asked ("a stone", "2 lb", "half a dozen", "3"); if your shop sends goods out, set deliver true on a line the customer wants sent to their house, such as goods too large or too many to carry away; the shop's courier takes those up at the time given, so you don't make them up, pack or carry them; quote a whole shopping list in one call. Each line gets its own offer (a quote-id) or refusal, announced at the counter. At your counter you can use this on your speaking turn in a conversation, before your line; then tell the customer the prices in your reply. Quote before you promise goods or give a price: there is no fixed catalogue, no bargaining and no credit. The customer accepts with buy, and chooses then whether to take the goods away or leave them at your shop, paid for; goods left at your shop are shown in your view as waiting-for the customer: give them to whoever collects them, or, if your shop sends goods out, send them up with send-up. A delivery line is paid in full when bought, with no bill to send after. This does not charge the customer.
