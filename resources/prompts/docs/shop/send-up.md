Send goods up to {{home}} from your shop, open or not, whether or not the customer is here: your shop's courier takes them, and there is nothing for you to make up, pack or carry. Use it when someone at the house has asked for goods to be sent up, or you've promised to send them. items is goods left waiting at your shop (marked waiting-for someone in your view), up to {{max-lines}} ids: they're paid for already, and within-hours (1 to {{send-up-max-hours}}, default 1) is when they arrive. goods is new goods, up to {{max-lines}} lines, each with item and amount as asked (such as more than you could sell over the counter), with for, the id of the person they're for: they're checked like a quote, given their own delivery time, and charged to the household's account at once, so send only what the house asked for, at the house's expense. Each line is sent or refused, and whoever they're for is told. At your counter you can use this on your speaking turn in a conversation, before your line.
