Send goods up to {{home}} from your shop. Your shop's courier will take them up without any intervention from you. Use it when a delivery from your shop is requested or promised. `items` is goods left waiting at your shop (marked `waiting-for` someone in your view), up to {{max-lines}} ids: they're paid for already, and `within-hours` (1 to {{send-up-max-hours}}, default 1) is when they arrive. `goods` is new goods, up to {{max-lines}} lines, each with item and amount as asked (such as more than you could sell over the counter), with for, the id of the person they're for: they're checked like a quote, given their own delivery time, and charged to the household's account at once, so send only what the house asked for, at the house's expense. Each line is sent or refused, and whoever they're for is told. At your counter you can use this on your speaking turn in a conversation, before your line.
