For a tradesperson, a craftsman or a merchant rather than staff, give `trade` instead of `position`: the trade or service wanted, in your own words (at most {{max-trade}} characters, such as "a piano tuner" or "someone to sweep the chimneys"). It may be someone already working nearby or someone from elsewhere. You and the recipient both learn a contact id, to use with `send-for`. Call `recommend` with `trade` before naming anyone: until it names someone, your character knows of nobody for the trade.
