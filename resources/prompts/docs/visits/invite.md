Invite someone to the house for a date and time. by: "in-person" (they're here with you), "post" (you're at a post office) or "messenger" (via: the id of anyone here who will carry it, the person invited included). who: their id, anyone you know, including people you've met who are away now (away-acquaintances). Or, instead of who, person: who they are, in your own words (at most {{max-person}} characters). Whenever you know their name, give it, with how they stand to you ("my father, Sir Henry Vane", not just "my father"): a name makes it certain the right person comes. A description without a name is still accepted. date ("1904-10-21") and time ("19:00"): when they're expected at the house. stay: how long, in your words ("to dine", "a week"). reason: optional, why. bedroom: optional, a bedroom id for a guest who stays the night.
