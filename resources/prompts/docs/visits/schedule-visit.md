Arrange a visit to {{home}} at a date and time. who: the id of anyone in the world; someone about is told at once, in their calendar, and makes their own way, and someone away arrives at the meeting place at the time, told nothing before. Or, for someone new from elsewhere, give instead of who: name, sex ("male" or "female"), age, from (where they live), known-as (a public description with nothing private, "{{known-as-example}}") and situation (second person: who they are, and how they stand with {{household-name}}); they're written now and arrive at the time. known-to: ids of anyone else who should have it in their calendar (default nobody).
