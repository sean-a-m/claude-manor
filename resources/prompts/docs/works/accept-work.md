Accept the price of a work on offer between you and someone else, when the other side set it: the worker accepts an order or the orderer's revised price, the orderer accepts the worker's revised price.

- `id`: the work's id under `works` in your view.

It's paid at once from the account named in the offer: the price leaves it, the materials are there, and the labour (price less materials) goes to the worker's purse. Nothing more is owed for it: don't `pay` for it again. The work is then the worker's to carry out. You must be together.
