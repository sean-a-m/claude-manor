Carry out a work you've accepted: spend the rest of this turn, and the time its current stage still needs, doing it. It ends your turn, like `spend-time`.

- `id`: the work's id under `works` in your view (yours, accepted).

Do it where the work is: for a move or removal, where the thing is now. If you stop early, the time you've put in counts, and `carry-out` again goes on with what's left. What comes of it is seen when the work is done.
