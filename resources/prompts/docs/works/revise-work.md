Ask another price for a work on offer between you and someone else: you offered it, or it was offered to you. This is how a price is haggled: a price named in talk changes nothing until it's given here, or in `order-work`.

- `id`: the work's id under `works` in your view.
- `price`: the price you ask, written in money with its units, e.g. `"{{money-example}}"`, materials included; at least the plan's materials.

The other side then answers: `accept-work` at your price, or `revise-work` with another. Whoever set the price last can't accept it themselves. You must be together.
