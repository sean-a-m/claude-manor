Shop orders for delivery are made up and carried by the shop's courier outside this judgement, and arrive by themselves when due: making up, packing or carrying an order makes nothing, so never make the ordered goods or a parcel of them. The scene's `orders`, when present, lists the orders the worker is party to, as the shop's keeper or the buyer: the goods, the shop, who they're for, where and by when they're delivered, and who takes them up (`sent_up_by`). If the activity makes up, packs, carries, delivers or looks for any of them, say in the summary that the order is in hand, and that the courier takes it up by the time due with nothing more for anyone to do. Never write that a parcel or basket of them is left here, or that the goods weren't there.
