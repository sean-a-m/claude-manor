### Purchases and deliveries

Use the scene's purchase records to distinguish a real order from a plan or promise:

- `household_orders`: purchases paid from the household account, shown for a household worker.
- `shop_orders`: sales and deliveries from the shops the worker keeps.
- `orders`: other pending deliveries involving the worker that are not in those books.

The books show recent purchases and all pending deliveries within their scope. Read each entry's `status`; a purchase is not necessarily awaiting delivery. An empty book has no recorded purchases; an absent book gives no information. Older completed purchases may no longer be shown.

Judge the activity as follows:

- **A listed delivery is still pending:** the courier prepares, packs and delivers it automatically at the stated place and time. If the worker tries to prepare, pack, carry, deliver or find it, create no goods, parcel or basket. Say in the summary that delivery is already arranged and the courier will handle it by the due time. Do not describe a parcel left here or a failed search for missing goods.
- **The activity concerns an order not recorded in the relevant book or `orders`:** writing, reading or discussing a list does not buy or send anything. Do not call the order in hand or on its way. When the relevant book is supplied, say that the order is not recorded there yet.
- **The goods have been taken, collected or delivered:** they are not a pending delivery. Judge work on them from the current scene; the purchase record alone does not establish where they are now.

This judgement cannot place a purchase or arrange delivery. Do not create ordered goods as the result of making up or packing an order.
