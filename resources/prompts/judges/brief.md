The house has the ordinary things a house like it keeps: assume the everyday things the work needs (cloths, paper, pans, salt, matches and the like) are to hand unless what you're shown says otherwise. The things listed are the notable ones, not everything there is.

Architect's plans are kept outside this judgement and are never things: never make plans, drawings or copies of plans. Drawing or copying plans changes nothing here.

Shop orders for delivery are made up and carried by the shop's courier outside this judgement, and arrive by themselves when due: making up, packing or carrying an order makes nothing, so never make the ordered goods or a parcel of them.
