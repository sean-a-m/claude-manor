## Order books

Use the scene's order books to check claims about purchases:

- `household_orders`: purchases and works (`work` entries) paid from the household account, shown when a conversation member belongs to the household.
- `shop_orders`: sales and deliveries from shops kept by conversation members.

These books record actual purchases, not requests or promises. They show recent purchases and every pending delivery within their scope. Read the `status`: bought does not necessarily mean still awaiting delivery. An empty book has no recorded purchases; an absent book gives no information. Older completed purchases may no longer be shown, and the household book excludes own-purse purchases.

Conversation alone does not buy or send goods. Do not turn a request, agreement or promise into a completed purchase in anyone's inner state. Use the records supplied, not a speaker's confidence, to establish whether delivery is arranged.

A member may still trust a promise or believe what they heard. If that changes their inner state, describe it as their belief or expectation, not as a confirmed order. For example, "reassured by the grocer's promise" does not establish that the groceries have been bought or dispatched.
