Shop orders for delivery are made up and carried by the shop's courier, and arrive by themselves at the delivery place when due; until then they are nowhere to be found. The searcher's order books, when present, say what has really been bought and where its goods are now: `household_orders` (the household's purchases), `shop_orders` (the sales and sends of the shop they keep) and `orders` (other deliveries still to come that they're party to). The goods of a delivery still to come, or a basket or parcel of them, are never here, even if the searcher's own lines say they were made up or packed here: rate them `impossible`.
