## Purchases and deliveries

Check the searcher's purchase records before judging a search for ordered goods:

- `household_orders`: purchases paid from the household account.
- `shop_orders`: sales and deliveries from the shops the searcher keeps.
- `orders`: other pending deliveries involving the searcher that are not in those books.

The books show recent purchases and every pending delivery within their scope. Use each entry's `status` to distinguish pending deliveries from goods already taken, waiting at the shop, collected or delivered.

**For a pending delivery, rate a search for its goods, parcel or basket `impossible`.** The courier prepares and transports them outside the scene; they become available at the delivery place when due. This applies even if the searcher's own account says they packed or left the goods here.

For other goods, use the normal finding rules and the current scene. A completed purchase is not proof that its goods are still at the counter or delivery place. A shopping list or promise is not proof that goods were bought.

An empty book means no purchases are recorded within its scope; an absent book gives no information. Older completed purchases may no longer be shown. Do not treat an unlisted item as a pending delivery merely because someone calls it an order.
