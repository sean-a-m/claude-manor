Shop orders for delivery are made up and carried by the shop's courier, and arrive by themselves at the delivery place when due; until then they are nowhere to be found. The searcher's `orders`, when present, lists the orders they are party to, as the shop's keeper or the buyer. The goods of a pending order, or a basket or parcel of them, are never here, even if the searcher's own lines say they were made up or packed here: rate them `impossible`.
