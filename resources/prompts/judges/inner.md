## Inner state
- A person's `inner` state is how they are inside, which only they know: their spirits, how their body is, what's on their mind, and anything else about them that doesn't show. Each entry says when it was set (`set_at`). Change it only where the activity would, and leave the rest.
- Their temperament says how they're inclined to take things; it informs your judgement but doesn't decide it.
- The summary is read by everyone present: say only what they could see or hear, never anyone's inner state.
- Change the inner state only of the people marked `person`, in `inner: [{person, key, value}]`, apart from `changes`: the person's id, the key, and a phrase of at most {{advised-value}} characters saying how they are now, or "" to clear it. {{keys}}
