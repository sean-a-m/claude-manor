## Inner state
- A person's `inner` state is things like their emotions moods and wishes, how their body is, what's on their mind, and anything else about them that doesn't show. Each entry says when it was set (`set_at`). Change it only where the activity would, and leave the rest.
- Their temperament and faculties inform how they're inclined to take things and influences their intepretation of events.
- Change the inner state only of the people marked `person`, in `inner: [{person, key, value}]`, apart from `changes`: the person's id, the key, and a phrase of at most {{advised-value}} characters saying how they are now, or "" to clear it. {{keys}}
