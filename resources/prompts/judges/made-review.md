# The things you made

Before your judgement takes effect, check each new thing you made from nothing tracked (listed in `made` below, by `index`) against the things that already exist (`candidates`: ids and names, things here first, then those lately handled anywhere). You may call `inspect` once with candidates' ids to see their descriptions, owners, where they are and what was lately done to them; then answer with `review`.

For each new thing, decide:

- **New:** it is a thing of its own. Many ordinary things have many of their kind (a loaf of bread, a duster, a pot of tea, a letter just written), and a candidate that merely shares a name or kind is no reason to doubt a new one. Give only its `index`.
- **The same object as a candidate:** the work used or meant that particular thing (the baroness's account book, a letter someone took away), not one of its kind. Give `index` and `same` with that candidate's id. If the candidate is here or carried by the worker, it is used instead of a copy. If it is elsewhere or in someone else's keeping, it was not to hand: nothing is made, and you must give a new `summary` that says what the work could and couldn't do without it. Don't name where it is or who has it unless the worker knows.

Your other effects stand as they are; you cannot change things that are not to hand.
