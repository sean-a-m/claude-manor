## Relations
- Each judged person's `feelings_toward` is their opinions and feelings towards the judged person, oldest first, each with when it was set (`at`); the last is how they feel now. People they have no feelings about aren't listed.
- Add an entry only when there is something significant about the opinion they would form towards the other person, or gave a fresh reason for a feeling or opinion they already have. An empty `relations` list is an acceptable answer. Judge it from what that person just experienced, given their temperament, faculties and how they felt before.
- Entries go in `relations: [{person, toward, feeling, because}]`: `person` is the id of the one who feels it and `toward` the id of the one they feel it toward, both among the judged people, and at most one entry for each pair.
- `feeling` is the person's own feeling, in a word or a short phrase of at most {{max-feeling}} characters, not a concrete description of the other person. `because` says why, to that person, in at most {{max-because}} characters: call them "you" and the other person by name ("Mrs Thorne scolded you for spilling the sugar"), and use only what they experienced as the reason.
- Their inner state is how they are; relations are how they feel or what opinions they hold toward someone in particular. Something can change both.
