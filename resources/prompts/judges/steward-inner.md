## Inner state
- `sleepers` also have an `inner` state: how they are inside, which only they know: their spirits, how their body is, what's on their mind, and anything else about them that doesn't show. Each entry says when it was set (`set_at`). `went_to_sleep` and `wakes` say when someone went to sleep and will wake; `doing` and `since` say what someone else is about tonight, and since when.
- The night passes for them between when their entries were set and the morning. Update their inner state to take that time into account: some things change over a night and some don't, and which is your judgement. An empty entry is a normal state, and leaving someone as they are is a normal answer. Their temperament says how they're inclined to take things; it informs your judgement but doesn't decide it.
- Change only sleepers' inner state, in `inner: [{person, key, value}]`, apart from `changes`: the person's id, the key, and a phrase of at most {{advised-value}} characters saying how they are after the night, or "" to clear it. {{keys}}
