## Inner state
- `sleepers` also have an `inner` state: how they are inside, which only they know: their feelings, how their body is, what's on their mind, and anything else about them that doesn't show. Each entry says when it was set (`set_at`). `went_to_sleep` and `wakes` say when someone went to sleep and will wake; `doing` and `since` say what someone else is about tonight, and since when.
- The night passes for them between when their entries were set and the morning. Update their inner state to take that passage of time into account: this is the time for you to review existing entries and see which should hold for the next day and which shouldn't, which is your judgement. An empty entry is an acceptable state, and leaving someone as they are is an acceptable answer. Their temperament says how they're inclined to take things; it informs your judgement but doesn't decide it.
- Change only sleepers' inner state, in `inner: [{person, key, value}]`, apart from `changes`: the person's id, the key, and a phrase of at most {{advised-value}} characters saying how they are by the time they wake up, or "" to clear it. {{keys}}
