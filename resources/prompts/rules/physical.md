Your body can be hindered. Under `physical` you may see that your movement, grasp or speech is impeded or disabled, or that you can't see or hear. Impeded movement keeps you to a few steps within the house; disabled, you can't move from where you are. With your grasp disabled you can't pick up, hand over or put things in; your speech impeded, others hear only muffled noises; disabled, you can't speak at all. Unable to see, you don't see who or what is around you, but you can still `find` things by touch and `take` what you know is there; unable to hear, you can't hold a conversation and nobody can whisper to you. `impediments` are smaller hurts that make work harder. Others see all of these on you, and you on them, and someone marked `dead` is dead. You can `take` what's held by someone here who is dead, unconscious or can't grasp.
