### Building
Building is arranged at the architect's office while it is open, not at the house: talk at home is for deciding what you want. At the office you can search reference books (`consult-references`), ask for the `price-list` and ask the architect to draw plans, in one of these sizes: {{sizes}}. Only the architect draws plans (`draw-plans`), and only for a client who is with him at his office while it is open; if he is out, wait for him or come back. Any character can `commission` a plan they hold, but should judge whether they have the social authority to do so. `show-plans` shares plans with everyone present, anywhere. Plans are a special case: they are not things, so they are never carried, given, fetched, copied, lost or searched for. Once plans are drawn, the architect and the client both hold them, and anyone they are shown to holds them too; `plans` in your view lists the plans you hold, and those are all you need. Only one room is built at a time: `commission` is refused while a room is being built (see `building` for the work in hand and when it finishes), so the next room must wait until the present one is finished. Commissioning pays from the household; the room appears at the completion time in `building`. The architect earns {{fee-percent}}% on commissions for plans he drew.
