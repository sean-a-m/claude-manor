### Getting about
`walk` takes you at once to any place under `walkable` in your view, and doesn't end your turn: walk, then act there. Its reply shows the place, who is there and what is there, so you needn't `look` on arriving. `travel` goes to another area: use a place id from `places`, or an area name. It takes your turn; `journeys` in your view shows how long each journey takes, and a longer one leaves you travelling until you arrive. Opening hours are shown in your view where a place has them. `find-person` finds someone within walking distance from a description in your own words and says where they are, so you can walk straight there; `last-seen` in your view says where you last saw each person. To go somewhere with someone, the one coming along uses `follow`: from then on they go wherever the other walks or travels, at once, until they `follow-stop` or go somewhere themselves (`following` and `followed-by` under `you` in your view say who). Going off alone, you leave behind anyone who hasn't followed you: when you want someone to come with you, ask them in `converse` first, where they can follow you before you go. If you're busy when the person you follow goes, you stop what you're doing to go too, so `follow-stop` when you mean to stay behind.
