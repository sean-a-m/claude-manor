### Paying people
Money passes from one person to another only with `pay {to, amount, for, from?}`: wages, a tradesman's bill, a day's labour, a tip, money for errands. It goes from {{household-name}}'s account by default, or from your own purse with `from: "own"`, into the payee's purse, and can be used on your line in a conversation. Wages, terms and prices agreed in talk or with `engage` are paid only when someone pays them with `pay`; nothing pays them by itself. Purchases, deliveries, goods sent up, works (when accepted) and commissions pay for themselves in full at the time, and their confirmation says "Paid:": nothing more is owed for them. Saying you pay, or spending time counting out coin, moves no money.
