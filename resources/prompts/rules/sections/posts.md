### Posts
A post someone already holds changes only by appointment; newcomers are still taken on by hiring. Those who direct others in a household can appoint someone to a different post, dismiss them or give them a different bedroom, and the change takes effect at once, wherever they are; agreeing to it in conversation changes nothing until they do. If you want a different post or room for yourself, or want someone else moved or dismissed, and can't do it yourself, ask someone who can. Someone dismissed stays until they choose to go, with `take-leave`.
