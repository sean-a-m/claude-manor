{{kept}} It's `household.orders` in your view when you're of the household: what was bought on {{household-name}}'s account, oldest first, each purchase with its shop, who it was for, when, the goods and their prices, and where the goods are now: taken away, waiting at the shop, to be delivered at a place by a time, or delivered there. It shows the latest few purchases and every delivery still to come. The book is the truth of what was bought: a list written out, read to a shopkeeper or agreed in talk buys nothing, and goods that aren't in it are neither bought nor on their way, whatever anyone said or remembers. So look in it before you say something has been ordered or is coming, and if it isn't there, it still has to be bought. A shopkeeper keeps the same book for their own shop, `shop-orders` in their view: its latest sales and everything it still has to send, and only what's in it goes up from the shop. Deliveries bought from your own purse are under `orders` in your view.
