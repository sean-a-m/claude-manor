#### Checking purchases and deliveries

The order books in your view record actual purchases. Check them before saying goods have been bought or are on their way.

- `household.orders`: purchases paid from the household account, visible to household members.
- `shop-orders`: sales and deliveries from the shops you keep, including customers' own-purse purchases.
- `orders`: other pending deliveries you are involved in that are not in a book you can see, usually purchases from your own purse.

Each book shows recent purchases and every pending delivery within its scope. Read each entry's `status`: goods may have been taken away, be waiting at the shop, be due for delivery, or have been delivered. A purchase listed in a book is not necessarily still on its way.

Talking about an order, writing a shopping list, or receiving a quote does not buy anything. Complete the purchase with `buy`, or, as the shopkeeper, arrange delivery with `send-up`. Check the tool result for accepted and refused goods.

If a delivery is not listed in the relevant book or `orders`, do not assume it has been arranged. An empty book means no purchases are recorded for that account or shop; a missing book tells you nothing about it. Older completed purchases may no longer be shown.

For a listed pending delivery, the courier handles preparation, packing and transport automatically. You do not need to make up the order or carry it yourself.
