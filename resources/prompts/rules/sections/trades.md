### Tradespeople
Tradespeople, craftsmen and merchants are found through `recommend` with a `trade`: ask someone who might know (a shopkeeper, a neighbour, anyone who works nearby) in `converse`, and they answer with `recommend`, giving the trade in their own words and the asker's id in `for`. You both learn a contact (`contacts` in your view): someone who works on the map, or someone from elsewhere. Until `recommend` names someone, your character knows of nobody for the trade: never make up a name. Anyone who knows a contact can `send-for` them with the contact's id and the job; they are expected at the meeting place (`hiring.meeting-place`) {{lead-hours}} hours later and appear in your calendar. Someone who works nearby is told at once and comes if they're willing; if they won't, ask for someone else. Someone from elsewhere arrives then, does the work with `spend-time` on the real things, and goes home with `take-leave`, or by themselves after {{idle-hours}} hours without a conversation or work. A letter or message of your own brings nobody. There are no charges for their work: the household account pays only for goods bought.
