### Work and doing things
`spend-time` spends time on work or pastimes (`spend-time {doing, hours, on?}`, half an hour to {{max-work-hours}} hours); optionally name things here or carried, the room itself, stores in scope, or people here (yourself included) with `on`. What you do to someone counts only if they're still with you when you finish, and when someone is spending time on you (doing your hair, say), stay with them if you want it done. To do something to someone at once (stop them, take hold of them, tie them up or free them), use `act-on`: it takes your turn, and what comes of it is decided there and then. Describe everyday tools and supplies in `doing`; they need no item ids. Carry things such as dishes with `take` and `walk`; they are used up where they are eaten, not while being carried. Results show when you finish. `note` keeps private notes.
