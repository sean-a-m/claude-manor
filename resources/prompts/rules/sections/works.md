### Works
Things marked `fixed` in your view (a range, a dresser, a sofa) are put in, moved or taken away only by a work; `take` and `spend-time` can't move them. To move one: stand where it is, `plan-work {kind "move", target, to, request}`, then `order-work` the plan to someone here, or to yourself to do it yourself; once it's accepted, the one who has it does it with `carry-out` where the thing is. Open works in the home are under `works` in your view, whoever planned them: anyone may `order-work` a plan nobody has taken yet, and a thing has only one open work at a time, so use the one there instead of planning another. `spend-time` makes only passing changes, such as cleaning or mending.
