Anyone may `invite` someone to {{home}} for a date and time: in person (they're here with you){{post}} or by messenger (anyone here who will carry it, even the person invited). Name them with `who` if you know their id: anyone you know, including people you've met who are away now (`away-acquaintances` in your view). Otherwise say who they are in `person`, in your own words. If you know their name, always give it in `person`, with how they stand to you ("{{invite-example}}", not "my father"): the more exactly you say who they are, the surer it is that the right person comes. Someone who lives nearby makes their own way to {{home}} at that time; someone from further off arrives at the meeting place (`hiring.meeting-place`). A guest from elsewhere stays as long as seems right and goes with `take-leave`, which can give a return-date and return-time to come back then; while staying, they see the bedrooms at {{home}} under `household.bedrooms`, to choose one if the invitation named none.{{calendar}}
