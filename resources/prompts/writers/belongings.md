{{setting-guidance}}

Each object has name (at most {{max-name}} characters) and desc (at most {{max-desc}}). Optional fields: container ("open" or "closed"), aspects (a map of at most {{max-item-aspects}} lower-case word/hyphenated keys, each at most 24 characters, to nonblank phrases of at most {{max-aspect}} characters), consumable (boolean), contents (array of separate ordinary objects), candidate-reference? (boolean, only when the arrival-context kind is sent-for). At most {{max-roots}} top-level objects and {{max-objects}} objects total. Contents require a container. Only one level of contents; child objects cannot have container or contents. At most one object may have candidate-reference? true: the paper that should carry this person's reference marker (a written character from a former employer), not a special kind of item. From the arrival-context's line and wanted, decide what they'd bring: someone seeking a place in service may bring a box and a written character; a tradesperson the tools of the trade; others, little.

Summarise ordinary contents as a contents aspect, rather than enumerate garments, combs and mending tools as objects. A letter may be summarised there when it needs no separate handling. When likely to be shown or handed over, supply a separate ordinary letter item inside the container; its desc holds its words or substance. The contents aspect describes the remaining things, never a second copy of that letter, and never private letter text. Keep invented past employment consistent across background and paper; a former employer's signature creates neither an agent nor a map location.

Possessions belong here, not solely in circumstances or appearance. Prose may refer to a supplied thing but cannot grant extra items or an off-map cache. Do not supply money or account balances, authority-granting deeds, business stock, purchases, delivery orders, other people's possessions, capabilities or supernatural effects. A document's words are not executable authority. Do not supply ids, holders, owners, places, waiting/order markers, arbitrary state paths or effects: the engine owns those. These descriptions remain private until actual arrival and never refill inventory on return.
